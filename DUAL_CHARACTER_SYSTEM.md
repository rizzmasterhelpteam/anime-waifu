## 3D Facial Integrity & Shading

Both 3D VRM avatars have verified facial rendering:
- **Seraphina (`seraphina.vrm`)**: Full anime facial features with violet irises, crystalline highlights, and delicate blush on peach-ivory skin.
- **Crimson (`companion_alt.vrm`)**: Spirited facial expression with vermilion ruby core irises, obsidian lash outlines, and alabaster skin.
- **No Face Occlusion**: Normalized world-coordinate outlines and MToon shading factors ensure facial meshes remain illuminated across all dynamic animation cycles (tea sip, catwalk walk, thinking, and celebration).

## How to Switch Models in the Dashboard

1. Open the extension popup menu.
2. In the **Model Selector**, switch between:
   - **Seraphina (Royal Gown)**
   - **Crimson (Gothic Lolita)**
3. Toggle between **3D VRM** and **2.5D Animated Sprite** render modes at any time.
